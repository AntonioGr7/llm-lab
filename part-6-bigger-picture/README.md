# Part 6 — Bigger Picture

What separates someone who ran the code from someone who can operate in this field professionally.

By the end of Part 5 you'll have done everything a small-scale version of the modern LLM stack can teach. This Part is about what to do with that knowledge — how to keep up with a field that moves weekly, how to extrapolate the things you learned at a tiny scale to the things labs do at a huge one, and what comes next in a career that's about to span the next decade of this technology.

This Part is short, conceptual, and easy to skip. We strongly suggest you don't.

## Modules

- **[21 — Reading the Literature](21-reading-the-literature/)** — How to read a paper in 30 minutes and extract what matters. A curated reading list, as queryable data. How to tell a genuine advance from noise. Finding the seminal papers in any area via the citation graph. Reproducing a result as the most important skill nobody teaches.
- **[22 — Scaling Intuitions](22-scaling-intuitions/)** — A working scaling-law calculator: the `6ND` rule, compute-optimal allocation, predicting loss before you spend a dollar. What changes and what doesn't when you scale. Why the $50 course prepares you for the $50M run. What labs actually hire for, and which next project signals real understanding.

## What you'll be able to do at the end of this Part

- Stay current with the field on a few hours per week, not full-time.
- Walk into an interview at a frontier lab and discuss tradeoffs in their stack credibly.
- Choose your next personal project with intent.
